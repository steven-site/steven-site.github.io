About Me

Hi, I'm Steven! I'm an incoming Assistant Professor in the School of Computer Science at Carnegie Mellon University. My research lies at the intersection of Software Engineering and Artificial Intelligence, where I combine core software engineering principles with modern AI, especially large language models (LLMs) and agentic systems. Here is also a short video about my research interests.

Previously, I was a PhD student at UIUC advised by Lingming Zhang.

Before that, I completed my undergrad at the University of Toronto where I was fortunate to work with Natalie Enright Jerger, David Lie, Nicolas Papernot, and Shurui Zhou.

🎓 Prospective PhD applicants

I am actively recruiting PhD students to start in Fall 2027. If you are interested and have background in relevant areas (e.g., coding agents, software engineering, model training, security, formal verification, alignment, and systems), please apply to the CMU SE PhD program and list me as a potential advisor (deadline: December 9th).

Note that I am mainly affiliated with the Software and Societal Systems Department (S3D) at CMU, so applying there and listing me will allow me to access your profile. You may also choose to apply to the Language Technologies Institute.

Feel free to email me with the subject [Prospective PhD Student], your CV, and a few sentences on what you would like to work on. Due to volume, I may not be able to reply to every email but I try to read every single one.

💻 Undergraduate & master's students

I am happy to work with motivated undergraduate and master's students who are currently enrolled in CMU on research projects.

If you are interested, email me with the subject [Undergrad/Master's Research], your CV, and a short note on your background, what you would like to work on, and how many hours per week you can commit.
